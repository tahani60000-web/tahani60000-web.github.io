About

I deliver scheduled pipelines with monitoring, retries, and alerts — not one-off scripts. Data lands clean in PostgreSQL, MongoDB, S3, Google Sheets, or CSV, with code your team can maintain.

I work with AI teams building training data and RAG pipelines, e-commerce teams tracking prices and catalogs, and research, real estate, and recruitment teams that need alternative data.

How I work

  1. You send the target URLs and the exact fields you need.
  2. I test feasibility and tell you whether it is possible, and what it costs, before you commit.
  3. You get a sample batch so you can check the data is right.
  4. Then the full build: scheduled runs, monitoring, documentation, handover.
